The profile

Who should you introduce to BSTS?

A growing small-to-mid-sized organization, roughly 20–250 employees, that handles customer, proprietary, or regulated information.

Often missing internally

  • An AI team
  • A cybersecurity team
  • A compliance function

Often present

  • Repetitive administrative workflows
  • Disconnected applications
  • Employees experimenting with AI
  • Rising customer security requirements
  • Security questionnaires arriving
  • SOC 2 pressure from a large customer
  • Government contracting ambitions
  • Real concern about AI adoption risk

Common sectors — not a limit

B2B SaaS · professional services · government contractors · health technology · regulated technology · any organization holding sensitive customer information

The engagement path

1 Discover — understand the business first. Your client leaves with a prioritized roadmap either way.
2 Implement — build one high-value thing and put it into production, tested before it touches live data.
3 Govern — write down how it is supposed to work.
4 Assure — prove it works, on an ongoing basis.

BSTS does not provide accounting, audit, legal, or tax services. BSTS does not issue SOC 2 reports. SOC 2 examinations and attestation reports are performed by qualified independent CPA firms. BSTS sells no licenses and takes no vendor commissions.
