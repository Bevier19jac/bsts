Scaling100% / Actual size — do not fit-to-page or shrink to printable area
Edge-to-edgeBorderless / full-bleed if the machine offers it
FinishingTri-fold, letter fold (roll fold) — not Z-fold
The one thing that must not be wrong: flip on the SHORT edge.
Landscape duplex flipped on the long edge prints the inside upside down relative to the outside, and the whole run is scrap. If the form says “head to head” vs “head to toe,” pick the one that keeps both sides reading the same way up in landscape.

Please do

  • Print a single proof sheet first and fold it by hand.
  • Check the front cover ends up on the outside right.
  • Keep the dark background rich — no toner-saver / draft mode.

Please don’t

  • Scale, rotate, or “fit to printable area.”
  • Print single-sided or flip on the long edge.
  • Z-fold instead of roll fold.
  • Substitute gloss or cover stock.
